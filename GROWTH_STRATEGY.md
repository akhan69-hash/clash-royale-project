- Deck archetypes
- Counter suggestions

- Personalized deck coaching recommendations
- Hidden card synergies (underrated combos)

**Expected Result:** 5-10% free users convert to pro (~50 paying users, $250/month)

## Phase 3: Coach Recruitment (Months 6+)

### 3.1 Coach Personas & Recruitment Channels

**Persona 1: Aspiring Coaches (Months-Old Players)**
- Skill: 5000-7000 trophy range
- Motivation: Extra income ($300-500/month)
- Channel: Discord, Reddit (r/ClashRoyaleBusiness if exists)
- Pitch: "Earn $20-40/hour teaching other players"

**Persona 2: Professional Streamers**
- Skill: 7000+ trophies, large audience
- Motivation: Diversify income beyond streaming
- Channel: Direct outreach (DM, email)
- Pitch: "Your audience trusts your expertise. Get paid for it."

**Persona 3: Content Creators**
- Skill: YouTubers, TikTokers with CR audience
- Motivation: Monetize existing following
- Channel: Brand partnerships, sponsorships
- Pitch: "Monetize your CR channel by coaching"

**Persona 4: Fiverr/Freelance Coaches**
- Already offering coaching elsewhere
- Motivation: Better platform, higher rates
- Channel: Direct recruitment from competing platforms
- Pitch: "Keep 80% instead of 50%"

---

### 3.2 Recruitment Tactics

**Tier 1: Self-Service Signup**
- "Become a Coach" button on homepage
- Simple onboarding: (bio, rate, availability, bank account)
- Instant approval (no manual review initially)
- Coach profile live within 1 hour

**Tier 2: Direct Outreach**
- Identify top 100 CR creators on YouTube/Twitch
- Personalized email: "We want to list you as a coach on Royale IQ"
- Offer: $500 signing bonus to first 20 coaches
- Include: How to set rates, sample students, payout examples

**Tier 3: Community Recruitment**
- Weekly "Coach Spotlight" on Twitter/Discord
- Feature new coaches with quotes: "Why I started coaching on Royale IQ"
- Referral program: Coaches who recruit 2 friends get $100

**Tier 4: Influencer Partnerships**
- Partner with 5-10 mid-tier streamers ($5-10K YouTube subs)
- They become coaches + promote the platform
- Revenue split: 40% platform, 60% influencer for first 3 months
- Each brings 50-100 students to platform

---

### 3.3 Coach Success Program

**Onboarding:**

## Coaching Session Workflow (User Experience)

### Student Side:
1. Browse coaches (sorted by rating, rate, availability)
2. Click "Book a Session"
3. Choose time from coach's calendar
4. Duration: 30/60/90 min (different rates)
5. Pay via Stripe checkout
6. Receive Zoom/Google Meet link
7. After session: Rate coach (1-5 stars + review)

### Coach Side:
1. Set availability (recurring schedule + exceptions)
2. Set rates (e.g., $50/hour for 1-on-1)
3. Receive booking notifications
4. Send meeting link 24 hours before
5. After session: Can also leave notes on student
6. Monthly payout to bank account (automatic)

- Average Coach Rating: Goal >4.8/5
- Revenue Per Coach: Goal $300-500/month

**Weekly Tracking Dashboard:**
- New signups (free & pro)
- Pro cancellations + reasons
- Coaching bookings + revenue
- Coach inquiries + approvals
